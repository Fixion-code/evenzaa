## 3. High-Quality Backlink Acquisition Strategy

Avoid paid link farms and private blog networks (PBNs) which trigger Google spam penalties. Instead, acquire authoritative, contextual links from real event partners:

### A. Vendor & Venue Cross-Links ("Staffing Partner")
- [ ] **Wedding Decorators & Caterers in Jaipur:** Reach out to local decorators, sound/light vendors, and caterers you've worked with. Ask them to add Evenza under their recommended "Staffing & Hospitality Partner" vendor list.
- [ ] **Heritage Venues:** Request listings or mentions on event resource pages of private palaces and heritage properties in Jaipur, Kukas, and Udaipur.

### B. College Societies & Volunteer Authority (Talent Side)
- [ ] Partner with student cultural societies at University of Rajasthan, Manipal University Jaipur, Amity Jaipur, and JECRC.
- [ ] Get listed on student placement/internship portal pages as an official event partner, linking to `/join-our-team` and `/volunteer-in-jaipur`.

### C. Press Releases for Milestone Deployments
- [ ] When staffing major sports marathons, large political rallies, or high-profile literary festivals, distribute a press release to local news portals (Patrika, Bhaskar, Jaipur Dialogues) highlighting the operational crew scale (e.g., *"Evenza deploys 150+ trained youth crew for Pink City event"*).
